Notation is $^A_Z X$
where:
$X$ = chemical symbol of element
$A$ = nucleon number (no. of protons + no. of neutrons)
$Z$ = proton number

$A=Z+N$ if and only if $N$ = the neutron number

Equations for decay processes **must balance** in terms of both $A$ and $Z$.
